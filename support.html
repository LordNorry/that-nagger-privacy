That NAGGER Support

Support and privacy contact for the That NAGGER Android app.

Email support
that.nagger.app@gmail.com

In-app support

That NAGGER includes Help and Send Feedback screens inside the app. Send Feedback can be used for bug reports, suggestions, general support, and privacy or data-deletion requests.

Privacy

Read the That NAGGER Privacy Policy.

Data-deletion requests

That NAGGER does not use app accounts and does not maintain a cloud copy of ordinary Nag data. For a feedback submission stored by the developer, request deletion by emailing that.nagger.app@gmail.com and include enough information to identify the submission.

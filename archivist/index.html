That ARCHIVIST Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026
Privacy and support contact: that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com

That ARCHIVIST is a local-first Android app for organizing belongings, records, documents, attachments, and related personal information. It is designed to keep ordinary archive content on the user's device rather than sending that content to the developer.

Information stored locally

That ARCHIVIST stores archive categories, groups, item records, notes, dates, history, settings, Protection Profiles, protected Groups/Categories, and related metadata locally on the Android device.

Vault content is encrypted at rest. Deeper Protection Profiles use separate protection keys and credentials. Passwords and protected content are not sent to the developer, and there is no developer/server recovery path for user-created protection credentials.

No app account, username, email sign-in, or cloud account is required to use That ARCHIVIST.

Attachments, photos, and files

When the user chooses to attach a file or take a photo, That ARCHIVIST uses Android's system file picker or camera access for that user-requested action. Attachments are stored locally with the archive and are encrypted at rest.

That ARCHIVIST does not automatically upload the user's archive records, photos, documents, or attachments to the developer or to a cloud service.

Camera permission

Camera permission is optional and is requested only when the user chooses a feature that takes a photo for an attachment. The camera is not used for advertising, analytics, or background surveillance.

Biometric authentication

If enabled by the user, That ARCHIVIST can use Android biometric/device authentication to help unlock the local app or protected content. Biometric templates are handled by Android and are not received or stored by the developer.

Feedback

The optional Send Feedback feature transmits information only when the user chooses to submit feedback. A submission includes the feedback category and message and may also include:

  • an optional reply email address; and
  • app version/build, Android version, and device model if the user selects the option to include app and device information.

Feedback is sent over HTTPS to a developer-operated feedback service. Service providers used to transmit, process, or store feedback may process the submitted information on the developer's behalf.

The feedback feature does not intentionally collect advertising IDs, Android account identifiers, serial numbers, precise location, contacts, or persistent device fingerprints.

Feedback and an optional reply email may be retained as long as reasonably useful for development, support, abuse prevention, or recordkeeping. A user may request deletion of a feedback submission by emailing that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com and providing enough information to identify the submission.

Google Play purchases

Optional Small, Medium, and Large Thank You purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment transaction. That ARCHIVIST receives purchase-related information needed to complete and consume the purchase, such as purchase state, product information, and a purchase token.

After a successful Thank You purchase, That ARCHIVIST stores local purchase counters on the device. The developer does not receive or store the user's payment-card details.

Thank You purchases are optional, do not unlock app features, do not remove ads, and are not subscriptions.

Information That ARCHIVIST does not use

That ARCHIVIST does not include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, analytics SDKs, social tracking, or an app account system. It does not intentionally collect location, contacts, microphone recordings, browsing history, advertising IDs, or persistent device fingerprints.

Internet access

Internet access is used for optional feedback submission and Google Play Billing. Ordinary archive content does not require an internet connection and is not transmitted to the developer as part of normal archive use.

Backups

Android automatic app-data backup is disabled for That ARCHIVIST. The app does not automatically send vault contents or attachments to the developer for backup.

Data retention and deletion

Ordinary archive data is stored locally on the user's device. Users can edit or delete records and attachments within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its ordinary app-private local data subject to Android and device behavior.

Because That ARCHIVIST does not maintain a developer-hosted account or cloud copy of ordinary archive data, the developer generally does not possess a server copy of the user's vault to delete.

For feedback retained by the developer or service providers acting on the developer's behalf, users may request deletion by emailing that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com and providing enough information to identify the feedback submission.

Security

That ARCHIVIST uses local encryption for vault data and protected content, HTTPS for transmitted feedback, and Google Play Billing for Thank You purchases. User-created protection credentials are intentionally not recoverable by the developer.

Children

That ARCHIVIST is not designed for children under 13 and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when That ARCHIVIST's features or data practices change. The policy linked from the current Google Play version should reflect the behavior of that release.

Privacy contact

Privacy questions, support requests, and feedback-deletion inquiries may be sent to that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com.

That Volumancer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026
Privacy and support contact: that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com

That Volumancer is designed to keep ordinary app data on the user's Android device and to avoid unnecessary collection, tracking, accounts, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, and advertising.

Information stored locally

That Volumancer stores volume profiles, schedules, activation history, appearance choices, launcher-icon choices, app preferences, and related configuration locally on the Android device.

No account is required. That Volumancer does not upload ordinary profiles, schedules, history, settings, or appearance choices to the developer.

Backups

Android automatic app-data backup is disabled for That Volumancer. The app instead provides user-controlled backup and restore.

When the user creates a backup, the backup contains app settings, profiles, and schedules needed for restoration. The user chooses where the backup file is saved through Android's document picker. That Volumancer does not automatically upload backup files to the developer.

Feedback

The optional Send Feedback feature transmits information only when the user chooses to submit feedback. Depending on what the user enters or selects, a feedback submission can include:

  • feedback category;
  • feedback message;
  • optional reply email address;
  • optional app version and build number;
  • optional Android version; and
  • optional device manufacturer/model.

App and device information is included only when the user selects the option to include it. Feedback is sent over HTTPS to a developer-operated feedback service. Service providers used to transmit, process, or store feedback may process the submitted information on the developer's behalf.

That Volumancer's feedback feature does not intentionally collect an advertising ID, Android ID, serial number, account identifier, or other persistent unique device identifier.

Feedback and an optional reply email may be retained as long as reasonably useful for development, support, abuse prevention, or recordkeeping. A user may request deletion of a feedback submission by emailing that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com and providing enough information to identify the submission.

Google Play purchases

The optional Small, Medium, and Large Thank You purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment transaction. That Volumancer receives purchase-related information needed to complete and consume the purchase, such as purchase state, product information, and a purchase token.

After a successful Thank You purchase, That Volumancer stores local counters on the device. The developer does not receive or store the user's payment-card details.

Thank You purchases are optional, do not unlock app features, do not remove ads, and are not subscriptions.

Information That Volumancer does not use

That Volumancer does not include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, analytics SDKs, social tracking, or an app account system. It does not intentionally collect location, contacts, photos or videos, microphone recordings, browsing history, advertising IDs, or persistent device fingerprints.

Permissions and Android system access

That Volumancer uses Android permissions and special access needed for its volume, profile, scheduling, quick-access, and restore-after-reboot features. Depending on the device and enabled features, this can include internet access, audio-setting control, notification-policy access, system-setting access, boot-completed handling, and exact-alarm scheduling.

Permission and special-access state is controlled by Android and can be changed in system settings. That Volumancer uses these capabilities to perform app functions and does not use them to build advertising profiles.

Data retention and deletion

Because That Volumancer does not create user accounts or maintain a cloud copy of ordinary app data, uninstalling the app removes ordinary local app data subject to Android and device behavior. User-created backup files remain wherever the user chose to save them until the user deletes those files.

For feedback retained by the developer or service providers acting on the developer's behalf, a user may request deletion by emailing that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com and providing enough information to identify the feedback submission.

Security

That Volumancer uses HTTPS for transmitted feedback and relies on Google Play Billing for Thank You purchases. Ordinary profiles, schedules, history, and settings are kept locally on the device unless the user explicitly creates a backup file.

Children

That Volumancer is not designed for children under 13 and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when That Volumancer's features or data practices change. The policy linked from the current Google Play version should reflect the behavior of that release.

Privacy contact

Privacy questions, support requests, and feedback-deletion inquiries may be sent to that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com.

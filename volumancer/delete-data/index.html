That Volumancer — Data Deletion Request

Last updated: September 25, 2026

How to request deletion

Email that.apps.caboodle@gmail.com with the subject That Volumancer data deletion request.

Please include enough information to identify the feedback submission you want deleted, such as the reply email address used, approximate submission date, and a short description of the feedback.

What can be deleted

That Volumancer does not create user accounts or keep a cloud copy of ordinary profiles, schedules, settings, activation history, or appearance choices.

The developer can delete feedback submissions that were sent through the optional Send Feedback feature, including:

  • feedback category and message;
  • optional reply email address;
  • optional app version/build, Android version, and device manufacturer/model included with the submission.

What is kept locally on the device

Ordinary app data is stored locally on the Android device. Users can remove it by deleting it within the app where applicable, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling That Volumancer.

User-created backup files remain wherever the user chose to save them until the user deletes those files.

Google Play purchase records

Optional Thank You purchases are processed by Google Play. Payment and transaction records maintained by Google are governed by Google's own policies and retention requirements. That Volumancer does not receive or store payment-card details.

Retention

Feedback may be retained as long as reasonably useful for development, support, abuse prevention, or recordkeeping unless a valid deletion request is received. Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping purposes.
